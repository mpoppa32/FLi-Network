Intelligence Briefing
Mission Briefing — FLiIntel Platform
01 / 04
01 — Platform Overview
This is not a CRM.
It's a mission-critical BD intelligence command center.
What it does
Connects what your SOCOM contact said in a meeting three months ago to the RFP that dropped on SAM.gov this morning.
What it's not
Not a contact database. Not a pipeline spreadsheet. Not a reporting tool. It thinks. It surfaces. It acts.
Every conversation is captured. Every relationship is mapped. Every signal is surfaced. The platform's job is to make sure you never walk into a meeting without knowing exactly where you stand — and never miss a pursuit because you lost track of a contact.
02 — The Foundation
Everything connects to the entity graph.
People, organizations, meetings, opportunities, and commitments are all linked entities — not siloed records.
◉
PEOPLE
PM, CO, COTR, Director. Tiered T1–T3 by relationship depth.
◈
ORGS
Primes, agencies, partners. Color-coded clusters on the network map.
◆
OPPS + MTGS
Every pursuit and every conversation — linked to the people who matter.
Key insight: When you log a meeting with Tom Chen at DARPA and link it to your Phase II pursuit, FLiIntel connects Tom's stance, his commitments, your open action items, and the solicitation timeline — automatically. No manual linking.
03 — Core Workflow
Sense → Decide → Act.
1
Log every meeting
After every call, site visit, or informal conversation — capture it. Attendees, key quotes, stances, commitments, action items. 5 minutes. The intelligence compounds.
2
Check Intel Station daily
Your command center. Cooling relationships, overdue commitments, urgent actions, and your BD rhythm — all surfaced without you having to look for them.
3
Execute the Agentic Loop
Every URGENT and HIGH action surfaced in Intel Station is a decision point. Click through to the entity. Act. Mark complete. The platform tracks what moves the pipeline.
04 — First Actions
Your first 5 actions.
Complete these to get your intelligence baseline running.
Build your network. Go to Network Map → Add your T1 contacts, their orgs, and key government nodes.
Log your first meeting. Use the + Meeting button. Capture attendees, key takeaways, and at least one action item.
Add your active opportunities. Go to Opportunities → Add each pursuit with agency, vehicle, and current stage.
Run your first Ask FLi query. Try: "Who are my warmest contacts and what opportunities are they connected to?"
Scan SBIR for your keywords. Go to SBIR → enter your core capability keywords → run the SAM.gov scan.
Access this briefing anytime from the ? help button in the nav bar.

02 / The State of Defense BD

The most consequential business development work in the world is running on the same tools as a regional insurance sales team.

Defense BD manages relationships across DoD, the Intelligence Community, and the primes that compete for $437B in annual contract obligations. Every program is the product of an eighteen-month capture cycle that turns on what was said in a single meeting and what shifted in a single budget mark.

That work runs on Outlook, Excel, Salesforce, and the operators' own memory. The platforms commercial sales sunset a decade ago are still where defense capture is staked.

Defense BD has been waiting twenty years for software that takes the work seriously.
03 / Doctrine

Positions we will not soften.

01Position

Relationships are intelligence.

Every conversation with a program manager, every commitment from a contracting officer, every off-the-record signal from a teammate at a prime is structured intelligence. Treating it as a CRM contact field is throwing away the work.

02Position

Procurement is signal.

SAM.gov solicitations, FYDP marks, recompete cycles, and budget passbacks aren't background noise — they're the operational picture against which every capture decision is made. Anyone treating them otherwise is operating blind.

03Position

Capture is an operation, not a sales motion.

It deserves the same discipline as a J2 staff cell: structured collection, fused intelligence, decision support, and a rhythm of review. The fact that it has historically been run otherwise is an artifact, not a feature.
